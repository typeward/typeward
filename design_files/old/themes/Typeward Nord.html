On the Asymptotic Behavior of
Stochastic Gradient Flows
Marek Sokol  ·  Ananya Khanna  ·  Jun Tashiro
Department of Statistics · April 2026
Abstract
We study the long-time behavior of solutions to the stochastic differential equation governing gradient flows under additive Brownian noise. Under a logarithmic Sobolev inequality, we establish exponential convergence of the law to the Gibbs equilibrium in 2-Wasserstein distance, with a rate determined by the LSI constant. Our analysis extends to non-convex potentials and yields explicit constants depending only on the curvature lower bound and the temperature parameter.
1. Introduction
The convergence of stochastic gradient methods to stationary distributions has been studied extensively in the context of Langevin dynamics. Recent works of [12, 27] establish entropy decay under various functional inequalities. We unify these via a single proof.
Throughout, we denote by μt the law of Xt, and by π the Gibbs measure with density proportional to e−βf.
In Section 2 we state the main result; Section 3 develops the necessary functional-analytic machinery.
2. Main Result
Let f : ℝd → ℝ satisfy the ρ-LSI. Then for all t ≥ 0,
W₂²(μt, π) ≤ e−2ρt W₂²(μ₀, π)
The proof proceeds via Itô's formula and a Grönwall argument applied to relative entropy. A more general statement covering sub-Gaussian potentials follows from Lemma 3.4.
